Medical Guidelines

Clinical guardrails · Thyroid, Fatty Liver, Lumbar Spine

🔴 Thyroid (Hypothyroid · Thyronorm 12.5mg)
  • Take Thyronorm at 06:30 AM on empty stomach · Wait 45–60 min before eating
  • Zero Soy/Soya — blocks hormone absorption (soy milk, tofu, soya chunks ALL banned)
  • Cruciferous vegetables (cauliflower, broccoli, cabbage) — ALWAYS fully steamed/cooked to deactivate goitrogens
  • Avoid raw cruciferous in any form (salads with raw cabbage, uncooked cauliflower)
  • High selenium foods encouraged: eggs, brazil nuts (1–2/day max)
  • Recheck thyroid levels every 3 months
🟡 Fatty Liver (Early Stage)
  • Zero refined sugars, jaggery, glucose, fructose syrups
  • Zero processed foods, packaged snacks, bread, rice, wheat
  • Limit alcohol completely
  • Apple Cider Vinegar (with mother) 1 tsp in warm water every morning — aids liver detox
  • Prioritise natural fats (ghee, eggs, nuts) over refined seed oils
  • Turmeric + black pepper daily — potent anti-inflammatory for liver
🔵 Lumbar Spine (Mechanical LBP)
  • No heavy axial loading — barbell back squats & conventional deadlifts BANNED
  • Core stability work (Cat-Cow, Bird-Dog, Dead Bug) before every gym session
  • Motorcycle commuting: use lumbar support cushion, limit rides >30 min
  • Sleep posture: side-lying with pillow between knees recommended
  • Desk setup: monitor at eye level, lumbar-supported chair
  • Glute activation (bridges) daily — weak glutes = primary cause of mechanical LBP
🌱 Daily Seed & Nut Allocation (30g MAX)

250g stock each. 30g combined daily cap to prevent caloric surplus.
